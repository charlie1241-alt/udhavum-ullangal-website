# Udhavum Ullangal Public Charitable Trust — Official Website

> **Modern, High-Performance, Mobile-First Web Experience for Udhavum Ullangal (UUPCT)**  
> Rebuilt with modern web standards, accessible design, corporate CSR partnership portal, and direct 80G tax benefit calculation.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Client Features

1. **Brand Identity & Header Alignment**
   - High-fidelity vector SVG trust logo with official emblem.
   - Clean top utility bar with verified contact details, 80G tax exemption badges, and CSR Registration Number (`CSR00004543`).
   - Non-wrapping, polished single-line desktop navigation bar with high-contrast sticky glassmorphism header.

2. **Interactive Flagship Services Slider**
   - Touch/drag and keyboard navigable slider showcasing 6 core service wings:
     - 🏥 **Health Care & Nellai Cancer Care Hospital**
     - 🎓 **Education & Mentorship Support**
     - 🌾 **Rural Upliftment & Disaster Relief**
     - 👩‍💼 **Women Empowerment & Vocational Training**
     - 👴 **Elderly Care & Support Initiatives**
     - ✨ **Anandha Deepavali & Festivity Drives**

3. **CSR Partnership & Corporate Sponsoring Hub**
   - Direct engagement for corporate CSR committees with Schedule VII alignment.
   - Downloadable governance proposals, audited financial disclosures, and interactive CSR pitch inquiry modal.

4. **Interactive 80G Tax Exemption Calculator**
   - Instant calculation of effective net giving cost under Section 80G (50% deduction on taxable income).
   - Direct NEFT / RTGS / IMPS wire transfer instructions for City Union Bank (Account: `510909010006725`, IFSC: `CIUB0000109`).

5. **Supporter & Milestone Ecosystem**
   - 15+ verified institutional and corporate supporters (Cognizant, TCS, L&T, Sundaram Finance, Apollo Hospitals, etc.).
   - 9 verified milestones across Tamil Nadu, spanning over 3 decades of humanitarian service.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic tags, OpenGraph & Schema.org JSON-LD microdata for rich search indexing.
- **Modern CSS3**: CSS custom properties (design tokens), mobile-first responsive flex/grid layouts, smooth animations, brand palette (`#19396D` Trust Navy & `#EC124F` Trust Crimson).
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Pure JS without heavy third-party framework dependencies for lightning-fast loading speeds (< 1.2s First Contentful Paint).
