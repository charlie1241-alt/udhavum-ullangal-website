Who We Are

Channeling the Goodwill of Communities & Bringing Hope to People in Need

"Our vision is to tap into the inherent selfless nature of humans to nurture, grow, and help fellow human beings during crises of any form. We envision building a selfless society that helps people in distress, poverty, and during a crisis."

A United Volunteer Force Dedicated to Direct Impact

Very often, members of a community want to help those in need, but do not know how they can contribute, or lack the bandwidth to manage complex interventions. At Udhavum Ullangal, we channel community goodwill, coordinating contributions to bring life-changing assistance directly to marginalized children, cancer patients, and destitute families.

At heart, we are ordinary citizens united by an extraordinary commitment. These disadvantaged individuals have hope for the future when their communities step forward together.
