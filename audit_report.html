AUDIT FINDINGS • PART 1
Critical Technical & Content Vulnerabilities

3 Critical Red Flags Identified on Current Site

The following anomalies were extracted directly from the live production site. These discrepancies directly hurt donor confidence and cause corporate CSR committees to question administrative quality:

1. Copy-Pasted Foreign NGO Platform Name ("betterplace.org")

CRITICAL RISK
Page: https://udhavumullangal.org.in/our-team/
Section: "What is our goal in the long run?" (Repeated in 2 columns)
Text: "...Above all you! You and the tens of thousands of world-improvers, who make betterplace.org grow and thrive."

Sponsor Due Diligence Risk: Betterplace.org is a German donation platform. When a corporate legal/CSR auditor reads another charity's name on the Trustee page, they suspect the website is plagiarized or unverified, instantly freezing grant discussions.

2. Commercial WordPress Theme Demo Image in Live Mission Slider

CRITICAL RISK
Page: https://udhavumullangal.org.in/about-us/
Slide 2 Image URL: https://financewp.themesflat.co/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/slide-overview02-1.jpg

Sponsor Due Diligence Risk: The mission carousel pulls stock images from an off-the-shelf theme vendor server ("Finance WP"). This signals incomplete work and lack of administrative care.

3. Misspelled Corporate Partner Brand ("Wipro" listed as "Wibro")

HIGH REPUTATIONAL RISK
Page: Homepage "Our Supporters" grid
Asset: partner15-wibro.png | Tag: alt="wibro"

Sponsor Due Diligence Risk: Corporates maintain strict brand guidelines. Misspelling a major technology corporate sponsor damages credibility and insults the sponsoring brand.

4. Disjointed Donation Journey (Redirects to Raw Bank Form)

HIGH CONVERSION LOSS
Action: Clicking "Donate" violently redirects off-site to:
https://formbuilder.ccavenue.com/live/city-union-bank/...

Sponsor Due Diligence Risk: Prospective donors are ejected to an unbranded, confusing bank form. There are no transparent giving tiers (e.g. ₹5,000 sponsors a student for 1 year), no explanation of impact, and no instant 80G tax receipt guarantee.

AUDIT FINDINGS • PART 2
Structural Deficits & Architectural Comparison

4 Structural & Governance Deficits

5. Complete Absence of a Dedicated Corporate CSR Section

MAJOR REVENUE BLOCKER

Why Corporates Cannot Proceed: CSR committees require structured Schedule VII alignment (Eradicating Hunger/Poverty, Promoting Education, Healthcare & Cancer Prevention). The website has no downloadable CSR Pitch Deck, no formal proposal request flow, and no case studies detailing corporate ROI (e.g. Accenture Keezhakundalapadi school building).

6. Missing Audited Annual Reports & Public Financial Statements

GOVERNANCE DEFICIT

Why Corporates Require This: Trust is built on audited numbers. The site only hosts 80G and 12A PDFs in the footer. It lacks downloadable Annual Reports, Form 10B audit summaries, and expenditure breakdown charts showing program vs. admin spending ratios.

7. Broken Navigation, Section Duplication & Obsolete Code

TECHNICAL DEFICIT

Specific Bugs: Broken link to /out-team/ on About Us; "Women Empowerment" button loops back to homepage; "What we do" heading is repeated twice on the homepage; copyright year stuck at 2024 wrapped in an invalid <h2> HTML tag; heavy preloader causing slow loading.
